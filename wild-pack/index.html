Wild Pack: Multiplier Run

Grow a pack. Pick your gates. Make it to the finish.

COMING SOON TO GOOGLE PLAY

About the game

Wild Pack is a fast one-thumb runner about growing a crowd. Steer your ranger down the trail and run your pack through the gates that multiply it. Slip through the wrong one and the pack shrinks. Every trail asks for a minimum pack at the finish line, so every choice counts.

How it plays

  • Drag to steer the whole pack left and right.
  • Gates add, subtract, multiply or divide your numbers. x3 is great. Divide by 2 hurts.
  • Dodge hazards on the trail: angry stumps, thorny bushes and moving traps.
  • Reach the finish with enough runners to clear the trail and move on to a harder one.

What you get

  • A pack that grows up to 300 runners on screen at once.
  • Trails that get faster and trickier as you advance.
  • 24 hand-made clay runner skins to unlock with the coins you collect.
  • Short, clean runs that fit a quick break. Play offline, with one hand.
Ads and privacy. The game has no accounts and no in-app purchases. It offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob; you can always play without watching any. For players aged 13 and over. Read the full privacy policy.
