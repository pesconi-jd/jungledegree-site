2. Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not collect your personal data ourselves.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information for that purpose (section 4).
  • Where the law requires it, ads are requested only after you answer a consent form. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent.
  • The game is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • your level, coin balance, and the skins you unlocked and equipped;
  • the player name you type in the menu;
  • your choices to turn sound and vibration on or off.

The game never sends this data to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. Please do not type personal information in the player name field.

The game can play sounds and make short vibrations. Both are optional and can be switched off in the menu. They do not record audio and do not collect any data; the game does not ask for access to your microphone.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you may choose to watch a video ad to get a reward (for example, to pass a trail). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. For this the game connects to the internet and uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When the game requests or shows an ad, the SDK may collect and use, under Google’s policies:

  • device and app identifiers, including the Android advertising ID (when allowed by your settings and consent);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device and the app (for example operating system version, app version, language);
  • ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance data (for example crash logs).

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the service working. Google processes it under its own privacy policy. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. We set the ad content rating to general audiences (PG) and we do not tag the game as directed to children.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The legal basis for the ad processing that requires your consent (for example, personalised ads, and access to the advertising ID in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time (section 5).

5. Consent and your choices

  • In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) on first use. Ads are requested only after the form allows it.
  • If the game shows a “Privacy Options” button in the main menu, you can use it at any time to review or change your consent.
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings > Privacy (or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • You can decline to watch ads. The game can be played without watching any.

6. What the game does not do

The game does not use analytics or tracking services of its own. It does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, photos or files. It does not sell personal data, and it has no social features or user-generated content. It has no accounts and no in-app purchases.

7. Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us at pesconi@jungledegree.com.

8. Retention and deletion

We do not hold personal data about you. Local game data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google’s own retention policies and controls (see the link in section 4).

9. International transfers

Because ads are provided by Google, the data described in section 4 may be processed by Google and its group companies in countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. According to Google, such transfers rely on safeguards required by data protection law, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision. See Google’s privacy policy for details. We do not ourselves transfer personal data outside the EEA.

10. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (withdrawal does not affect processing done before). To exercise these rights, write to pesconi@jungledegree.com. We will reply within one month of receiving your request; where the law allows, this period may be extended by up to two further months for complex requests, and we will tell you if so.

Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about ad data are best made to Google, through your Google account controls or the consent options in section 5. If you write to us, we will help you find the right place.

11. Complaints

If you believe your data protection rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Portugal this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), www.cnpd.pt. You may also contact the authority of your own country of residence.

12. Security

Ad requests are sent by Google’s SDK over secure connections. Local game data stays in the app’s private storage on your device.

13. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or external fonts, scripts or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; as with any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs when you visit a page. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

14. Changes and version history

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If we add features that use new data (for example analytics or purchases), we will update this policy before releasing them.
